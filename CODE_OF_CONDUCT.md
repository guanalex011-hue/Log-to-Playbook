# Code of Conduct

Log-to-Playbook is a small open source project, and we want it to stay useful,
welcoming, and safe for people learning and debugging under pressure.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about risks.
- Keep discussions focused on reproducible logs, playbooks, and safer diagnosis.
- Avoid sharing secrets, credentials, private logs, or personal data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Encouraging unsafe production changes without warnings.
- Repeatedly derailing issue or pull request discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block users who violate
these expectations. Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md`.
