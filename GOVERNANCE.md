# Governance

Log-to-Playbook currently uses lightweight maintainer governance.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

- Keep built-in playbooks safe by default.
- Review new playbooks for specificity, risk labels, and secret exposure.
- Keep releases versioned and documented.
- Prefer transparent decisions in issues and pull requests.

## Decision Process

Small changes can be accepted by a maintainer after review. Larger changes,
such as changing the matching model, release process, license, or public CLI
interface, should be discussed in an issue before implementation.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Consistent contributors may be invited as maintainers after demonstrating good
judgment around safety, review quality, and project direction.
